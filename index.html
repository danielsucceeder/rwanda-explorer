About Rwanda Explorer

Rwanda Explorer is a simple tourism assistant concept designed to help visitors discover attractions in Rwanda and, in the future, provide AI-powered assistance and translation support.

The long-term idea is a digital assistant that helps tourists find suitable destinations, understand the places they visit, and communicate across language barriers.

Note: This is a prototype developed as part of a university web development assignment. It is a frontend-only demo and does not include a real AI system.

Popular Attractions

A selection of must-visit destinations across Rwanda.

Volcanoes National Park

Home to endangered mountain gorillas and the Virunga mountains. Gorilla trekking through bamboo forest is Rwanda's most famous experience.

Nyungwe National Park

A lush tropical rainforest with incredible biodiversity, famous for its canopy walkway suspended high above the trees.

Akagera National Park

Savannah landscapes, lakes, and wildlife safaris — the only park in Rwanda where you can see the Big Five.

Kigali

Rwanda's clean, vibrant capital — full of culture, museums, local markets, and modern city life.
